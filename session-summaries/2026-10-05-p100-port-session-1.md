## Key decisions

- **CUDA 12.8** for `sm_60` (CUDA 13 dropped Pascal/Volta). P100 host driver R525–R580.
- **Build flags:** `NINFER_PRE_AMPERE_BUILD` (sm_60 + sm_70 shared SIMT routes),
  `NINFER_VOLTA_BUILD` (sm_70 Tensor-Core routes only), `NINFER_PASCAL_BUILD` (GP100 replacements).
  Volta Tensor-Core headers `#error` in Pascal builds, so a Tensor-Core kernel can never compile
  to an empty (silently wrong) sm_60 kernel. Unreachable Volta entry points of non-admitted paths
  are throwing stubs (`src/ops/volta_tc_sm60_stubs.cpp`), following the existing sm86-stub pattern.
- **Admission on SM60:** `qwen3.8-27b/nvfp4` (main) and `qwen3.8-27b/gguf-q4-k-m`, TP1/TP2,
  Text/None/MTP. Vision, DFlash, TP4, QUASAR, 35B and RAM-KV are rejected at startup.
- **Prefill GEMMs:** the "decode once + dense GEMM" routes are dual-arch through
  `src/ops/common/pre_ampere_gemm.cuh` (FP16 TensorOp on SM70 unchanged; FP32 SIMT SGEMM on SM60).
  GGML_K has its own chunked FP32 route (`ggml_k_cutlass_simt.cu`). FP32 operands double the
  prefill workspace relative to Volta.
- **Flash attention:** native Pascal kernel reading the paged INT8 KV cache in place, FP32, zero
  workspace (Volta's flash route stages ≈ 368 MB of FP16 KV at 180k context). Correction recorded:
  the inherited chunked fallback was slow, not a VRAM problem.
- **NVFP4 artifact is mixed-format** (NVFP4 MLP 0–55; row-FP8 attention/GDN/MLP 56–63/head/
  embedding; W8 MTP; Q4G64 draft head; BF16 GDN control) — Pascal needs SIMT routes for all of them.
